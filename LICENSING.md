from the copyright holder **before** such use begins. There are no exemptions,
trial periods, or automatic conversions.

Commercial use includes (but is not limited to):

- Deploying xWallet in a production environment as part of a paid product or service
- Offering xWallet as a hosted or managed service (SaaS, PaaS, etc.)

- Processing transactions or assets on behalf of paying customers
- Bundling or distributing xWallet with a commercial product
